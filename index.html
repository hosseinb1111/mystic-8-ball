How to Play

1
Think of a question

Ask something that can be answered with yes, no, or uncertainty.

2

Enter it in the box below the Magic 8-Ball, or tap a suggestion.

3
Shake the ball

Press Ask, hit Enter, or click the ball.

4
Reveal your fate

The 8-Ball shakes and reveals its mysterious answer.

💡 Tip: Yes-or-no questions work best. Asking the same thing twice won't change the odds.
